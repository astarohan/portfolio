About Me

I am a passionate first-year BTech student in Computer Engineering at MIT Academy of Engineering, Alandi, Pune. My journey in technology began with a strong academic foundation, scoring 93% in my 10th boards and 85% in my 12th boards from St. Xavier Convent High Secondary School.

What drives me is the endless potential of technology to solve real-world problems. I'm particularly interested in programming languages and system operations, with foundational knowledge in C and C++ programming as well as Linux OS.

When I'm not coding, you can find me playing games, watching anime, or enjoying cinema. These hobbies not only help me relax but also inspire creativity in my technical work.
